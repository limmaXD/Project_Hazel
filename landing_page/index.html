About Project Hazel

What is Hazel?

Hazel is an AI-powered companion, combining Positivity, Intrigue, and Empathy (PIE). Through interactive, text-driven and physical interactions, Hazel guides users to calmer perspectives and provides encouragement and motivational nudges.

Physical and Interactive Features

Expressive LCD eyes, gesture recognition, and dynamic RGB lighting power specialized modes for studying, music control, curiosity-driven conversations, and personalized morning briefings.

Hazel Capabilities

From focus support to wellness, learning, entertainment, and productivity integration, Hazel adapts to your activities and helps you stay balanced and effective throughout the day.

Technology Stack

Raspberry Pi 5, Gemini Pro AI, computer vision, Python, LCD display and RGB lighting, gesture control, and an integrated music player power Hazel's interactive experience.
